# Provisioning patterns to crib for the Ansible playbook

Resolves wayfinder ticket #7. Survey of what already provisions, checks or deploys a PROVES-adjacent station, so the playbook reuses it instead of reinventing it. Each piece is classified per ADR 0002: **station layer** (hardware, OS, rotator chain, tracking tools; same for every mission) or **mission pack** (flight dictionary, GDS framing plugin, key handoff, NORAD ID and TLE source).

## What this means for the playbook

- Two roles under one play, split along ADR 0002: `station` (OS guards, time sync, hamlib, udev by serial, rotctld unit, Python/uv, gpredict, kit tools, `station.toml`, self-check) and `mission_electra` (david-cubesat venv, dictionary and plugin pinned by sha256, GDS unit, TLE timer for one `CATNR`, key handoff procedure). `deploy.sh:9-12` is the seed list for both.
- Copy from satnogs-ansible: the skeleton, the guard asserts, the udev handler, the CI job. Do not copy its Docker, SDR or self-hosting machinery.
- Copy from proves-electra-ops: `portlib.sh` serial lookup, `self-check.sh` as the Provisioned gate, `station-check.sh` Pi health rows, `known-good.toml` as the manifest schema, `check-manifest.py` in CI.
- Copy from tinygs-tle-creator: the render-lint-install-reload-print discipline and the read-back assert for settings that can silently not apply.
- Everything in the second table is new work; the largest items are service units for rotctld and the GDS, and time sync.
